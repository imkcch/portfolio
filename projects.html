EmoScale: Zero-Shot Emotional Intensity Controlin Speech Synthesis via Diffusion Models

EmoScale is a zero-shot framework for emotional speech synthesis that enables fine-grained intensity control (e.g., making "happy" sound "happier") without requiring manual intensity labels. It combines self-supervised encoders with a diffusion-based decoder, using RankNet to learn relative intensity from prosodic features via pairwise ranking.

Towards Undetectable Audio Deepfakes with Fidelity Preservation

Audio deepfakes are increasingly realistic, but we lack methods to transform them into more natural-sounding speech. We showed that speech enhancement models that are trained only to remove noise, can make audio deepfakes sound more natural without any retraining. It's a proof that these models possess transferrable knowledge beyond their original task, opening new directions in adversarial audio research.
